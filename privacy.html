Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general-purpose draft and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Please have it checked against your local data protection laws (such as GDPR) before relying on it.

1. What information we collect

When you create an account, we collect: your first and last name, email address, password, date of birth, gender, country and city, and phone number. Your phone number is only shown publicly if you choose to make it visible.

When you publish a listing, we collect the listing details you provide (price, location, description, features) and any photos you upload. When you use the messaging feature, we store the messages you send so the conversation can be displayed to both participants.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information to create and secure your account, let you publish and manage listings, let other users search for and view listings, enable private messaging between buyers and sellers, and verify that account holders are at least 18 years old.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your name, profile photo, and (if you chose to show it) your phone number are visible to anyone viewing a listing you've published.
  • Your email address, date of birth, and gender are never shown publicly.
  • Messages you send are visible only to you and the other participant in that conversation.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider, which acts as our data processor. We don't sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

5. Cookies and local storage

LuciHome uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to keep you logged in between visits and to remember basic preferences. This is required for the site to function and isn't used for advertising or tracking across other websites.

6. Your rights

You can access and correct your personal information at any time from your profile page. You can permanently delete your listings, photos, favorites, and profile information by deactivating your account, also from your profile page. If you have questions about your data, contact us at the address below.

7. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you deactivate your account, your profile, listings, photos, and favorites are permanently deleted.

8. Children's privacy

LuciHome is restricted to users who are at least 18 years old. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to office@lucihome.com.
