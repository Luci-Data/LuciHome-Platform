Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LuciHome is currently in Beta / Early Access. This policy may be updated as new features are added before the official launch.

This Cookie Policy explains what LuciHome ("we", "us", "our") stores in your browser when you visit lucihome.com (the "Platform"), and why. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What we actually use

LuciHome does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics scripts. Instead, we use your browser's local storage — a similar technology to cookies, but readable only by our own site, never sent to other companies.

What's storedPurposeCan it be rejected?
Login session Keeps you signed in to your account between visits, so you don't have to log in every time. No — this is essential for accounts to work. You can log out at any time to clear it.
Cookie notice preference Remembers that you've seen and accepted this notice, so we don't show it again on every page. No — but you can reset it any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for their own marketing or advertising purposes — full stop. The only outside company involved is Supabase, our database and hosting provider, which stores your data on our behalf and does not use it for anything else. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

No advertising, no tracking

We don't run any advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or third-party analytics trackers on the Platform. If this ever changes in the future, this page will be updated first, and — where required by law — we'll ask for your consent before activating anything beyond what's described above.

How to control this

  • Clear your browser's local storage or site data at any time to remove everything described above (this will also log you out).
  • Adjust your browser settings to block local storage entirely; note this will prevent staying logged in to LuciHome.

Changes to this policy

As LuciHome moves from Beta / Early Access toward official launch, this Cookie Policy will be updated to reflect any new features. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
